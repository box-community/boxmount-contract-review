# Northstar Analytics Contract Playbook

This synthetic playbook is for the Box Mount demo. It is not legal advice.

## Review standard

Classify each material deviation as:

- **Critical** — must be resolved before signature.
- **High** — requires Legal and business-owner approval.
- **Medium** — negotiate when practical or record an explicit exception.
- **Acceptable** — consistent with the approved position.

For every deviation, cite the agreement section, summarize the issue, and propose replacement language.

## Approved positions

### Limitation of liability

- General aggregate liability must not exceed fees paid or payable in the preceding 12 months.
- Unlimited liability is acceptable only for fraud, willful misconduct, and obligations that cannot legally be limited.
- Confidentiality, data-protection, and indemnity exposure may be capped at two times the general cap.
- Any otherwise unlimited customer claim is **Critical**.

### Indemnification

- Indemnity must be mutual and limited to third-party claims.
- The indemnifying party must control the defense, subject to reasonable cooperation and settlement approval.
- One-sided indemnity covering first-party losses or all contract breaches is **High**.

### Customer data and AI

- Customer data may be processed only to provide, secure, and support the services.
- Customer data must not be sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose models without explicit written opt-in.
- A right to train models on customer data by default is **Critical**.

### Security incidents

- Confirmed incidents affecting customer data must be reported without undue delay and no later than 48 hours after confirmation.
- The supplier must provide ongoing updates, remediation details, and reasonable cooperation.
- No notification commitment, or a period longer than 72 hours, is **High**.

### Payment

- Standard payment terms are net 30 days from receipt of an accurate invoice.
- Net 45 may be accepted with Finance approval.
- Net 60 or longer is **Medium**.

### Renewal

- Renewal must require affirmative agreement or provide at least 60 days' advance notice with a practical opt-out.
- Automatic renewal with less than 30 days to opt out is **High**.

### Termination

- Either party may terminate for an uncured material breach after 30 days' notice.
- The customer should be able to terminate for convenience with 30 days' notice after the initial term.
- Supplier-only convenience termination is **High**.

### Governing law

- Preferred venue is California, New York, or the customer's principal place of business.
- Mandatory arbitration outside the United States requires Legal approval and is **Medium**.

## Required output

The review memo must contain:

1. An executive summary and overall recommendation.
2. A table of deviations with severity, section, concern, and proposed position.
3. Suggested replacement language for Critical and High findings.
4. Questions requiring human judgment.
5. A clear statement that the review is AI-generated and requires qualified legal review.
